About Writemygrants

A grant consultant who started as a grant reviewer at Innovate UK.

Writemygrants was built on a simple belief: good work deserves funding, and good funding shouldn't be out of reach.

Writemygrants — Founder & Grant Consultant
Professional background

Over four years inside the grants process, on both sides of it.

Before founding Writemygrants, I worked at Innovate UK, helping founders find the right grants and guiding them throughout the application process. I set up grant funding masterclasses to help demistify the grant application process from A to Z.

Writemygrants exists to help early stage founders have a roadmap and clear understanding of the public funding landscape in the UK. Today, I work as a freelance grant writer and funding strategist for innovative startups, small businesses and non profits who need an expert grant consultant without the overhead of hiring one in-house.

Why I started Writemygrants
Early in my career, I watched startups apply for every possible grant funding opportunity. I also realised that a lot of founders lacked the time to properly research and understand whether a grant was the right fit for them. I started Writemygrants for two reasons: saving founders time, and demystifying the application process. My 4+ years at Innovate UK made me an expert in public funding, and I want to share that expertise with passionate founders who need to accelerate their journey.
