Grant Writing Services

Support at every stage of the funding cycle.

Engage Writemygrants for a single application or a standing grants function. Every service below is available on its own or bundled into a full-service funding strategy.

01 / Grant Research

Grant research that finds the funders you actually qualify for.

Grant research is where most applications win or lose before a word is written. At Innovate UK, I spent years researching and signposting my clients to the right opportunities, matched to their mission, budget, sector, and geography. By doing so, I helped teams focus on opportunities worth pursuing, not long shots.

Benefits

  • Stop wasting hours scanning grant databases manually
  • Focus only on grants you're genuinely eligible for
  • Uncover funders outside your existing network

Deliverables

  • Ranked shortlist of matched grant opportunities
  • Eligibility summary and deadline calendar
  • Funder priorities and past-awardee notes

02 / Grant Writing

Grant writing that speaks the reviewer's language.

This is the core of Writemygrants: full narrative grant writing that translates your work into the specific criteria a funder is scoring against. Every proposal is written from scratch, grounded in your data, inputs and outcomes, and edited until the case is airtight.

Benefits

  • A proposal built around funder priorities, not templates
  • Clear, compelling narrative free of jargon and filler
  • Save the internal hours a strong application demands

Deliverables

  • Complete written proposal narrative
  • Logic model and outcomes framework
  • Two rounds of revisions before submission

03 / Proposal Development

End-to-end proposal development, not just a document.

A winning application is more than a narrative: it's detailed work packages, Gantt charts, risk registers, letters of intent and more. Proposal development covers the entire package, assembled and quality-checked against the funder's exact requirements.

Benefits

  • Nothing missing at submission — every attachment accounted for
  • A budget narrative that matches the story you're telling
  • One point of contact managing the whole package

Deliverables

  • Completed budget and budget narrative
  • Supporting documents checklist and collection
  • Final formatted proposal, ready to submit

A funding strategy that plans years, not just deadlines.

Grant funding compounds when it's planned. I build a multi-year funding strategy that sequences grant applications alongside your organisation's growth plans, cash flow needs, and capacity — so you're never scrambling to write a proposal in a week.

Benefits

  • A clear view of funding opportunities 12–24 months out
  • Diversified funding instead of reliance on one source
  • Realistic capacity planning around application deadlines

Deliverables

  • Multi-year funding roadmap and calendar
  • Diversification and risk assessment
  • Quarterly strategy check-ins
